Our Roots & Heritage

Building Confidence, Supplying Excellence

For over six decades, Sathik Groups has been more than just a supplier of Hardware & Plumbing materials. Sourced directly from authorized manufacturer plants and chosen with care, we have stood side-by-side with local families as they laid the foundation stones of their dream homes.

Our legacy originated in 1964 by Mohamed Ibrahim Sahib and Haji Shiek Abdul Kader, laying a solid foundation of trust, values, and client satisfaction. Carrying this legacy forward, Sathik Traders was originated on 2003 ,with Pumps and Plumbing materialsand followed by Sathik traders, on 2024 , Sathik Enterprises was originated withBath and Kitchen wares, expanding our services to offer complete hardware, plumbing, and sanitaryware wholesale & retail , Industrial material solutions under one roof.

In our traditions, a house is not merely concrete and steel—it is the sanctuary of your family's aspirations, a legacy built on hard work and shared dreams. We honor this sacred trust by offering transparent retail prices, genuine products, and personalized guidance that spans generations.
