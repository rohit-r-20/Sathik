Our Roots & Heritage

Building Confidence, Supplying Excellence

For over six decades, Sathik Groups has been more than just a supplier of building materials. Sourced directly from authorized manufacturer plants and chosen with care, we have stood side-by-side with local families as they laid the foundation stones of their dream homes.

Our legacy originated in 1964 by Mohamed Ibrahim Sahib, laying a solid foundation of trust, values, and client satisfaction. Carrying this legacy forward, Sathik Traders was originated on 2007 by Haji Shiek Abdul Kader, expanding our services to offer complete hardware, plumbing, and sanitaryware retail solutions under one roof.

In our traditions, a house is not merely concrete and steel—it is the sanctuary of your family's aspirations, a legacy built on hard work and shared dreams. We honor this sacred trust by offering transparent retail prices, genuine products, and personalized guidance that spans generations.
