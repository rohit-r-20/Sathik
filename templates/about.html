Our Story

Building Trust, One Quality Product at a Time

Sathik Groups was founded in 1964 with a simple promise: to help families and builders get genuine, high-quality materials straight from India's top brands at fair retail prices.

From our roots in door locks, hardware, and paints in 1964, we expanded into plumbing and pumps in 2003, and complete bathware in 2024. Today, our 3 specialized stores in Redhills offer everything you need under one roof with expert guidance.
